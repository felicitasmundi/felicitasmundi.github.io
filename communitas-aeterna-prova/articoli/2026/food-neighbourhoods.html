I Vicinati del Cibo · Food Neighbourhoods

Gabriele Dettori · fondatore FelicitasMundi

This article was first published in Italian by Sovranità Popolare: «I Vicinati del Cibo di Gabriele Dettori»

Finding people who speak the same language

The problem I have always felt, especially before taking an evolutionary leap in my life, was truly connecting with my own land, at home, in Olbia, with people who spoke my same language. I lived through a great and deep loneliness. Over time I discovered that many of us shared this difficulty: finding, in our everyday lives, something that nourished a deeper part of who we are.

In 2019, as a DJ, entertainer and event host, I saw something inside and outside of me: each of us is an individualised part of one consciousness, the Cosmic Consciousness. I came to it through a philosophical knowledge that masters have been sharing for thousands of years, the Vedas: in Sanskrit, the absolute knowledge of who we are in relation to the Cosmos.

By shifting my attention away from the ordinary world, I discovered that there are already active communities: spiritual, rural, farming communities. People who nourish the soul, and people who carry on the traditional and authentic knowledge of food, of producing, of woodworking, of craftsmanship. From there, five years ago, a need was born: why not think of one single technological tool that helps us not only to communicate, but to coordinate? The universe is ordered, and in Sanskrit that order is called Dharma.

The eternal community already exists

FelicitasMundi starts from a philosophical question: who we are, how to awaken our true talent, how to find the people walking the same path. To do something with a revolutionary flavour, we first need to grow the community, the eternal community that already exists around us. In Sardinia there are very many of us who still live by the morals our grandmothers taught us.

The Cervello Digitale Evoluto (Evolved Digital Brain)

There are a great many groups working towards the self-sufficiency of their own land. The difficulty is that what they share stays scattered across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, in the databases of other platforms. Beautiful content that can no longer be found a week later; hour-long videos of which people only read the title.

This is why we created a Cervello Digitale Evoluto, an Evolved Digital Brain: a technological system based on the interaction of local groups, rural and spiritual districts that cooperate and use the same database. Videos are transcribed and indexed by their most important themes, like an encyclopaedia, and every day a newspaper shows what is happening in each neighbourhood and in each local group. It is also a management system: each group sets its goals, who follows them, the deadlines, how many products it has available, at what price it sells them, how to get them to that district and how to let companies, associations and families know. An analytical index remembers who does what in each territory, and it is also printed: if the power went out tomorrow, we would know who is on the ground.

From guest to karma yoga

You start as a guest: you join the platform, you buy products and training, you come to the events. The next step is karma yoga, the yoga of action: acting without thinking of the fruit of the action, with the intention of working for others as an offering to creation. This is central today: a community is fed by foundations where you do not do things for your own immediate gain, and this inner direction changes you and the way you relate to others.

I Vicinati del Cibo

Think global, act local. Each rural district should lie within a radius of about 20 km from a pick-up point, where families, producers and companies come once a week for their products, ordered online or on the spot. We are 50 families in the Oristano neighbourhood and we know that every month we need rice and pasta: we can make agreements with producers for those quantities, even with discounts. The next step, which many already take, is to say: we have this need, we have uncultivated land, let us support those who produce, together, financially or with our own hands through karma yoga. We are not inventing anything new: we want to invite people to follow the example that so many already set.

When networks become a fabric run by a technological management system, we know what is available in each region and we can give real support to projects across regions. And as Guido Grossi says, it is important to move from raw materials to processed products, which can also be exported: in Sardinia we have crafts, textiles and dairy products with a history and an authenticity.

Doing things together again

We hold in our hands a technological tool that could let us do truly important things, for our own growth and for our collective growth, and instead we spend our time scrolling. That time and energy can become a resource for something virtuous. Instead of staying at home, you could go to a pick-up point and organise a jam session, a workshop, gatherings where people do practical things: children learning to work wood, young people writing and singing songs, people eating together and living the act of eating as something sacred. This is where togetherness is born, and it leads to training, work, production and distribution.

Where we are and how to take part

In Sardinia we are starting neighbourhoods in the Oristano area, in Quartu Sant'Elena, in Olbia and in the Sassari-Alghero area, and there are active groups in Florence, Bergamo, Cuneo and Ferrara. On 21 November in Baratili San Pietro, near Oristano, the first Felicitas Festival will take place. You can contact us to start a neighbourhood: whoever has land, whoever produces, whoever has vehicles and can take care of distribution. You can come to the Festival, join the neighbourhoods, open one and propose a festival in your area.

By uniting in a steady, clear and firm intention, we can truly change the course of things.
