Finding people who speak your language

For a long time, especially before a turning point in my life, I struggled with one thing: building real connections where I lived, at home in Olbia, with people who spoke my language. I went through a deep and profound loneliness. Over time I realised I was far from alone in this. Many of us find it hard to discover, in our everyday surroundings, something that feeds a deeper part of who we are.

In 2019, while working as a DJ, entertainer and event host, I had an insight about myself and the world around me: each of us is an individual part of a single consciousness, the Cosmic Consciousness. I came to this through a philosophy that teachers have passed on for thousands of years, the Vedas, which in Sanskrit means the absolute knowledge of who we are in relation to the Cosmos.

Once I looked beyond everyday life, I found that active communities already exist: spiritual, rural and farming communities. There are people who nurture the soul, and people who keep alive the traditional, authentic know-how of food, farming, woodworking and crafts. Five years ago this led to a question: why not create a single digital tool that helps us not just communicate, but work together in a coordinated way? The universe has an order, and in Sanskrit that order is called Dharma.

The eternal community is already here

FelicitasMundi begins with a philosophical question: who are we, how do we awaken our true talents, and how do we find the people on the same path? If we want to do something truly revolutionary, we first need to build community, the eternal community that already exists all around us. In Sardinia, a great many of us still live by the values our grandmothers taught us.

The Cervello Digitale Evoluto (Evolved Digital Brain)

Countless groups are working to make their local area self-sufficient. The problem is that what they share ends up scattered across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram and the databases of other platforms. Wonderful content disappears from view within a week, and hour-long videos get no further than their titles.

That is why we built the Cervello Digitale Evoluto, an Evolved Digital Brain: a technology platform where local groups and rural and spiritual districts work together and share the same database. Videos are transcribed and indexed by their key themes, like an encyclopaedia, and a daily newspaper shows what is happening in every neighbourhood and every local group. It is also a management tool: each group sets its goals, who is responsible for them and the deadlines, what products it has and at what price, how to get them to the district and how to reach businesses, associations and families. An index keeps track of who does what in each area, and it is also printed: if the power went out tomorrow, we would still know who is on the ground.

From guest to karma yoga

You start as a guest: you join the platform, buy products and training, and come to events. The next step is karma yoga, the yoga of action: acting without attachment to the results, with the intention of serving others as an offering to creation. This matters more than ever today. A community thrives when people do not act for immediate personal gain, and that inner shift changes both you and the way you relate to others.

I Vicinati del Cibo

Think global, act local. Each rural district should sit within about 20 km of a pick-up point, where families, producers and businesses come once a week to collect products ordered online or bought on the spot. Say there are 50 families in the Oristano neighbourhood, and we know we need rice and pasta every month: we can agree those quantities with producers, even at a discount. The next step, which many people already take, is to say: this is what we need, there is unused farmland, so let's support the producers together, with money or with our own hands through karma yoga. We are not inventing anything new. We simply want to invite others to follow the example that so many already set.

When these networks become one connected fabric, run by a digital management system, we know what is available in every region and can give real support to projects across regions. And as Guido Grossi says, it is important to move from raw materials to finished products, which can also be exported: in Sardinia we have crafts, textiles and dairy products with real history and authenticity.

Doing things together again

We carry in our pockets a technology that could help us do truly important things, for our own growth and for everyone's, and yet we spend our time scrolling. That time and energy could go into something worthwhile. Instead of staying at home, you could go to a pick-up point and organise a jam session, a workshop or a hands-on gathering: children learning woodwork, young people writing and singing songs, people sharing a meal and treating food as something sacred. This is how people come together, and coming together leads to learning, work, production and distribution.

Where we are and how to get involved

In Sardinia we are launching neighbourhoods around Oristano, in Quartu Sant'Elena, in Olbia and in the Sassari-Alghero area, and there are active groups in Florence, Bergamo, Cuneo and Ferrara. On 21 November, the first Felicitas Festival will take place in Baratili San Pietro, near Oristano. Get in touch if you would like to start a neighbourhood: if you have land, if you produce, or if you have vehicles and can help with distribution. Come to the Festival, join a neighbourhood, start one, or propose a festival in your own area.

If we come together around a steady, clear and firm intention, we really can change the course of things.
